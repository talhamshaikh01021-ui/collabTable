Campaign Brief

What we're looking for in creators.

Sakura House is looking for food creators who can authentically capture the experience of our authentic Japanese cuisine. We need creators who understand Japanese culture, can work in low-light traditional settings, and can produce both vertical video content (Reels, Shorts) and high-quality photography for our social channels.

Must-shoot References:

Sakura tree in full bloom Traditional tea ceremony Chef's counter sushi preparation Traditional izakaya atmosphere

Deliverables:

2 Reels (60-90s each) 5 Stories 3-5 High-res photos

Format Requirements:

Vertical 9:16, minimum 1080×1920, H.264 codec. Photos at 300 DPI minimum.
