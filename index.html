How a campaign runs

Five stages, one deal room

Every collaboration follows the same path, so both sides always know where a campaign stands.

  1. 01 Brief The restaurant describes the menu, the dates, the deliverables, and the budget range.
  2. 02 Match Creators apply from the campaign page, and the restaurant shortlists who fits.
  3. 03 Negotiate Offer and counter-offer settle the fee, scope, and shoot date before signing.
  4. 04 Escrow The restaurant funds escrow. The creator is paid only after the content is approved.
  5. 05 Deliver The creator delivers through the platform, and both sides confirm to release payment.
  6. Throughout Contact stays masked Phone, email, and handles unlock only after the contract is accepted.

The deal room
Things people ask

Before you start

It is a marketplace where restaurants post food-content campaigns and food creators apply. Both sides agree terms in a shared deal room, and payment moves through escrow rather than directly.

It depends on the brief. Every campaign publishes a budget range — for example ₹20K–₹50K — and the final figure is agreed between the restaurant and the creator in the deal room before a contract is signed.

After the contract is accepted, the restaurant funds escrow. The platform fee is deducted, and the creator receives the net payout once the delivered content is approved and the review step is complete.

Not before a deal is agreed. Phone numbers, emails, and handles are masked on both sides, and they unlock only once the offer and contract are accepted in the deal room. This keeps the campaign record complete and payment protected.

If escrow has not been funded, nothing moves. If escrow was funded and the campaign stops, the amount stays held until both sides confirm how it should be released.
