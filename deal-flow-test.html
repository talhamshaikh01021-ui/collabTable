The restaurant and the creator are two separate rooms in the product, which makes one hard thing to check: whether a move on one side actually reaches the other. Here both sides run on the same campaign in one page, so a counter, an acceptance and the payment are visible on both sides as they happen.

What to check

  1. Counter. Counter from either side. The other side should show the new number, swap to its waiting message, and grey out its Accept and Counter buttons until it answers.
  2. Accept. Accept on the other side. Both sides should return to live controls and the counters should read each side's figure.
  3. Pay. Pay Now appears for the restaurant only. It goes UNPAID → PENDING → PAID and the contract opens on both sides.
  4. Masking. Before payment, no name, email, handle, address or avatar appears anywhere in either room. After it, the contract shows them.
  5. Deliverables. Upload a file as the creator, then review it from the restaurant. The restaurant's single Request Edits request locks once used.
